Pokeshop

A private tool for one person's Pokémon card business.

Pokeshop runs on its owner's own home server and is used only by its owner. It tracks card stock and prices, and sends its owner alerts when a store opens a drawing, sends an invite or restocks a product.

With the owner's permission it reads, read-only, the store emails that a Gmail filter puts under one label, to spot invites and order confirmations. It is not offered to anyone else.
