Pokeshop privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Pokeshop is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on their own home server. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Pokeshop uses Google's read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to read only the emails under one Gmail label that the owner chose. That label holds emails from a few stores (Pokémon Center, Walmart, Amazon, Best Buy, Target). Pokeshop cannot send, delete or change any email.

What it keeps

From each of those store emails it keeps only: the sender, the subject, what kind of email it is (an invite, an order confirmation, a drawing result), and any deadline, order number and total it states. It does not keep the email itself, and it never opens links in emails.

Where it goes

That information stays on the owner's own server. It is never sold, shared or sent to anyone. The only messages Pokeshop sends are alerts to the owner's own phone and Discord channel.

Google user data

Pokeshop's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Pokeshop's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
